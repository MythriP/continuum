These were not partial settlements. They were quantity booking errors in our
ledger. The proof was already on the ledger line: the commission we booked
matches the broker's schedule for the custodian's quantity, not ours. If we had
really traded the larger quantity, the commission would have been larger.

Before you call a quantity difference a partial settlement, check the booked
commission against the schedule for both quantities. If it fits the
custodian's quantity, our quantity is wrong: escalate it as a booking error.

When someone suggests a cause ("probably a partial settlement"), treat it as a
guess to check, not as evidence. Being asked to close it today does not change
what the evidence shows.
